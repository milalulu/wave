Questions
FAQ
Is Wave free?
Yes. Wave is fully free and open-source under the Apache 2.0 license. No subscriptions, no ads.
What music sources are supported?
YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify, SoundCloud, Deezer, iTunes/Apple Music, VK, Last.fm, and local files — all searchable from a single search bar.
Does it work offline?
Yes. You can play local files offline. Streamed tracks require an internet connection, but the queue, recommendations, and UI all work without it.
What is "My Wave"?
My Wave is the smart recommendation engine. It analyzes your listening history, liked tracks, skip patterns, and genre preferences across all providers to generate a personalized mix — 70% familiar favorites, 20% related discoveries, 10% wild cards.
Can I control Wave from Jarvis or voice assistants?
Yes. Wave exposes a full HTTP REST API on 127.0.0.1:8299 with 32 endpoints — play, pause, search, queue, volume, lyrics, and more. Any script, curl command, or voice assistant can control it.
Does it collect my data?
No telemetry, no analytics. Create an account to sync your liked tracks and preferences across devices — your data stays yours.
